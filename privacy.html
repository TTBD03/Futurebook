Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Futurebook is a social website where people share posts with their friends. This page explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

Your name, email address, and password (stored securely, never in plain text). Everything you post, including posts, comments, likes, friend requests, and reports or blocks you make.

How we use it

To run Futurebook: to let you log in, show your posts to your friends, and keep the site safe. We do not sell your personal information.

Who can see your content

Your posts, likes, and comments are visible to you and your accepted friends. Your name can be seen by other signed-in members so they can find and add you.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with our service providers, who host the database and the website. They process it only to run Futurebook.

Your choices

You can delete your own posts at any time. To delete your account and data, or to ask a question about your data, email us at [tastytwistbd@gmail.com].

Children

Futurebook is not for children under 13. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will remove the account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
